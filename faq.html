Questions and answers

What guests usually ask before they come. If your question is not here, write to us.

How do we get from the port at Kamares to the apartments?

Kamares is about 25 minutes from Platys Gialos by car or taxi; there is also a municipal bus via Apollonia. Whether we can collect you from the ferry: To confirm.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out by 12:00. If your ferry arrives earlier or leaves later, tell us and we will see what we can do.

Do we need a car?

Not to enjoy Platys Gialos: the beach, tavernas and shops are a five-minute walk, and the bus to Apollonia stops at the beach. To explore the island, a car or scooter for a day or two helps; see getting around.

Is there parking?

Yes, private parking on site.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, in every apartment.

Is there air conditioning?

Yes, in every apartment.

What is in the kitchen?

Breakfast is not offered To confirm. There are cafés for breakfast at Platys Gialos and in Apollonia; see breakfast.

Do you have cots? Is it suitable for children?

The beach at Platys Gialos is sandy and shallow, which suits children. Cots and high chairs: To confirm.

Can we bring our dog?

Yes, pets are welcome. Please tell us when you book.

Where can we swim?

The sandy beach of Platys Gialos is a five-minute walk. Below the house there is also a rocky shore, about 60 m down, for quiet swimming in clear water; wear sandals on the rocks. See beaches for the rest of the island.

Are there shops and tavernas within walking distance?

Yes. Platys Gialos, about 250 m away, has tavernas, cafés and shops along the beach.
