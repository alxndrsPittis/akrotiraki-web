Practical

Questions and answers

What guests usually ask before they come. If your question is not here, write to us.

How do we get from the port at Kamares to the apartments?

Ferries from Piraeus reach Kamares in 2.5 to 5 hours; see the Piraeus–Kamares schedules on Ferryhopper or Openseas. If the crossing is part of the holiday for you and not just transit, take the slow conventional ferry: five hours of open deck, islands passing, and the Aegean doing what it does. Kamares is about 25 minutes from Platys Gialos by car. Take a taxi, or the municipal bus via Apollonia. Tell us your ferry time and we arrange a taxi.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out by 12:00. If your ferry arrives earlier or leaves later, tell us and we will see what we can do.

How does check-in work?

Check-in is self-service, with a key box. We send you the instructions before you arrive.

Do we need a car?

Not for Platys Gialos: the beach, tavernas and shops are a five-minute walk, and the bus to Apollonia stops at the beach. For the rest of the island, cars and scooters can be rented; see getting around.

Is there parking?

Yes, private parking on site.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, in every apartment.

Is there air conditioning?

Yes, in every apartment.

What is in the kitchen?

Each apartment has its own kitchen with a hob, a fridge, a kettle, a coffee maker and cooking utensils.

Are linen and towels provided, and how often are they changed?

Yes. Bed linen is changed weekly; towels are changed mid-week on request.

Is the apartment cleaned during our stay?

For stays of 7 nights or more, the apartment is cleaned once a week.

Is there a washing machine?

Yes, a washing machine is available on request.

Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is not served at the house, but Sifnos koulouria, local honey and coffee are waiting in the apartment when you arrive. Nearby at Platys Gialos: Palmira, Theros bakeproject and Way Cup.

Do you have cots? Is it suitable for children?

The beach at Platys Gialos is sandy and shallow. Cots and high chairs are available on request.

Can we bring our dog?

Yes, pets are welcome. Please tell us when you book.

Where can we swim?

The sandy beach of Platys Gialos is a five-minute walk. Below the house there is also a rocky shore, about 60 m down, for swimming. See beaches for the rest of the island.

Are there shops and tavernas within walking distance?

Yes. Platys Gialos, about 250 m away, has tavernas, cafés and shops along the beach.

How much does it cost?

Rates are seasonal. The request form shows the price for your dates before you send it.

How do we pay, and is there a deposit?

A deposit of 30% by bank transfer confirms the booking. The balance is paid on arrival, in cash or by bank transfer.

What is the cancellation policy?

The deposit is refunded for cancellations made more than 30 days before arrival; after that it is kept.

Are there quiet hours?

Quiet hours are 23:00 to 08:00.
