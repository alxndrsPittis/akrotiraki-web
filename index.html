Four apartments above the bay.

A whitewashed house on the small headland of Platys Gialos, Sifnos, with the sea below every terrace.

Akrotiraki

On a hill, a short walk from the beach.

Akrotiraki means “small headland”. On it stands a whitewashed Cycladic house divided into four apartments, two maisonettes and two studios, with stone terraces, pergolas and about 5,000 m² of garden.

We are a family from the island and look after the apartments ourselves.

The apartments

Two maisonettes, two studios.

Each has a sea view, a private terrace, air conditioning and Wi-Fi, and a welcome basket of local things.

The place

Shore, beach, village, hill.

The shore

About 60 m below the house is our own rocky shore, for swimming.

The beach

The long sandy beach of Platys Gialos is about 250 m away, a five-minute walk, with tavernas and shops along the water.

The island

Apollonia, the capital of Sifnos, is 15 minutes by car. Sifnos is reached by ferry from Piraeus.

An old hill

The hill of Akrotiraki is also an archaeological site. An Early Cycladic settlement and its cemetery, of the Early Bronze Age, were found here, together with some of the earliest securely dated evidence for silver production in the Cyclades.

Getting here
Ferry from Piraeus to Kamares, 2.5 to 5 hours depending on the boat (schedules for the Piraeus–Kamares route on Ferryhopper and Openseas). If the crossing is part of the holiday for you and not just transit, take the slow conventional ferry: five hours of open deck, islands passing, and the Aegean doing what it does. From the port, about 25 minutes by taxi, or by the municipal bus via Apollonia. Tell us your ferry time and we arrange a taxi.
