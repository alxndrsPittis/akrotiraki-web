Akrotiraki

A quiet hill, a short walk from the beach.

Akrotiraki means “small headland”. On it stand four whitewashed houses in the Cycladic way, with stone terraces, pergolas and about 5,000 m² of garden.

We are a family from the island and look after the houses ourselves. A line about the family, to confirm.

The place

Shore, beach, village, hill.

The shore

About 60 m below the houses is our own rocky shore, for quiet swimming in clear water.

The beach

The long sandy beach of Platys Gialos is about 250 m away, a five-minute walk, with tavernas and shops along the water.

The island

Apollonia, the capital of Sifnos, is 15 minutes by car. Sifnos is reached by ferry from Piraeus.

An old hill

The hill of Akrotiraki is also an archaeological site. An Early Cycladic settlement and its cemetery, of the Early Bronze Age, were found here, together with some of the earliest securely dated evidence for silver production in the Cyclades. Please treat the hill with care.
