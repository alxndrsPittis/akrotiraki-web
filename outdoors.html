Sifnos

Outdoors

Footpaths between villages and chapels, cycling, boats, and getting around.

Walking

Sifnos has a waymarked network of footpaths, Sifnos Trails: ten numbered routes from 2.4 to 15 km, with short variants. Each has its length, walking time and difficulty on the website. There is little shade on the higher paths; take water.

Four of the routes:

  • Trail 4: Cedars, Olive Trees and the Sea

    Platys Gialos – Profitis Ilias Kontou – Fykiada – Platys Gialos

    A loop from the last bus stop at Platys Gialos: 11.4 km, about 4 hours, steep in places. Variant 4A links Vathi to the loop (2.2 km).

  • Trail 2: Towards the Miraculous Saint

    Kastro – Faros – Chrysopigi

    5.8 km, about 2 hours 20 minutes. From Faros a coastal path continues past Glyfo and Apokofto.

  • Trail 1: The Eternal Capitals

    Agios Andreas – Kastro – Panagia Poulati – Artemonas

    8.6 km, about 3 hours, past the ancient acropolis and the old capital.

  • Trail 9: The Wuthering North

    Kambanario – Heronissos

    2.4 km, about 45 minutes, easy. The bus stop is just uphill from the beach at Heronissos.

At sea

Boats leave from the small marina at Platys Gialos, at the west end of the beach near our headland. You can rent a small boat for the day or go with a skipper, around the island or to the uninhabited islet of Polyaigos and to Kimolos.

Getting around

The ferry from Piraeus arrives at Kamares, the port. A municipal bus service links Apollonia and Artemonas with Platys Gialos, Kamares, Kastro, Faros, Vathi and Heronissos; the timetable changes with the season. There are few taxis on the island, so ask us to help you book one. Car and scooter rental:
