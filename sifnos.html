Sifnos

The island around us

A small island in the western Cyclades, reached by ferry from Piraeus. Apollonia, Artemonas and Kastro sit on the hills; the beaches, Platys Gialos among them, lie in the bays below. Sifnos is known for its potters and its cooking, and for a long history of mining and building.

These pages are our notes for guests: beaches, walks and boats, where we like to eat, and the archaeology of the island. They are not a complete guide, and things change from season to season.

Getting around

  • Ferry From Piraeus to Kamares, the island’s port, in 2.5 to 5 hours depending on the boat.
  • Bus A municipal service links the villages and the main beaches; timetables change with the season.
  • Car or scooter Rental agencies at Platys Gialos, Kamares and Apollonia.
  • Boat Boat rental and day trips from the marina at Platys Gialos.
